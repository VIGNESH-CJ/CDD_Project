FULL ARCHITECTURAL SPECIFICATION

10 Built-In Intelligent Modules

Every intelligent capability in AI Foodie is backed by real mathematical models, Scikit-learn regressors, recipe deduction matrices, and WebSocket protocols.

Module 1
Personalized Recommendations

Collaborative and content-based scoring engine analyzing order histories, customer ratings, and dietary profiles.

Module 2
Conversational Food Assistant

Real database-backed chatbot querying actual live menu items, filtering veg/non-veg and prices, and adding to cart directly.

Module 3
ML Food Demand Prediction

Scikit-learn RandomForestRegressor forecasting tomorrow's demand and optimal prep buffers based on historical orders.

Module 4
Smart Ingredient Inventory

Automatic recipe-based stock deduction on order confirmation with instant low-stock alerts and transaction ledger.

Module 5
Intelligent Kitchen Prioritization

Dynamic KDS queue ranking orders using waiting time, dish complexity, item count, and delay penalty thresholds.

Module 6
Smart Delivery Assignment

Driver dispatch optimization using Haversine distance calculations, real-time driver availability, and performance rating.

Module 7
Real-Time Order Tracking

Persistent WebSockets synchronizing customers, restaurant admins, kitchen staff, and delivery riders instantly.

Module 8
Dietary & Allergen Filtering

Safe filtering for Pure Veg, Jain, Vegan, Gluten-Free, and Dairy/Nut allergens verified strictly by recipe database.

Module 9
Restaurant SQL Analytics

Chart.js visualizations of daily, weekly, and monthly revenues, peak ordering hours, and best-selling menu items.

Module 10
Food Waste Reduction

Shelf-life and expiry risk monitoring (< 3 days), financial loss calculation, and AI special-offer suggestions to reduce waste.
